Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Zevada Legacy Consulting respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Zevada Legacy Consulting (“Zevada,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, disclose, and protect information through zlegacyconsulting.com, our consultation request form, our scheduling experience, and our website chat or concierge tools.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identifiers and contact information: name, company name, email address, and phone number.
  • Professional or project information: project type, general scope, services requested, preferred contact method, and information you choose to include in a message.
  • Appointment information: scheduling selections and information submitted through the consultation booking experience.
  • Communications: information you provide through the website chat or when you contact us.
  • Technical and usage information: information that may be collected by hosting, security, analytics, embedded-form, scheduling, or chat providers, such as IP address, browser/device information, referring page, and interactions with the website.

Please do not submit confidential project documents, trade secrets, financial records, worker personal information, or other sensitive project information through the initial website form or chat.

How we collect information

We collect information directly from you when you submit a consultation request, schedule a consultation, use the website chat, contact us, or otherwise choose to provide information. We may also receive technical information from the service providers that host or operate portions of the website experience.

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Schedule, confirm, and manage consultations.
  • Understand a prospective client’s general project needs.
  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve the website and related services.
  • Communicate about an inquiry or scheduled consultation.
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms, and protect rights, safety, and security.

How we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the website and business, including website hosting, form processing, appointment scheduling, chat or concierge functionality, security, and technical infrastructure providers. These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us and subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect the rights or safety of Zevada or others, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. We do not knowingly use this website to share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

California privacy rights

If the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, applies to our relationship with you, California residents may have rights to request access to or information about personal information, deletion, correction, and information about how personal information is collected and disclosed. Depending on the circumstances, California residents may also have rights to opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, and receive equal treatment for exercising privacy rights.

To submit a privacy request, email javzev66@icloud.com with the subject line “California Privacy Request.” Please identify the request you are making. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and we may deny or limit a request where permitted by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Cookies and third-party services

The website and embedded services may use cookies, local storage, scripts, or similar technologies for functionality, security, remembering preferences, attribution, or service operation. Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies. Third-party services embedded in the website may collect information under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to inquiries, providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
